AAR size is the ZIP download size; native and asset sizes are uncompressed. Final APK/AAB download size depends on ABI splits and app packaging. Android also copies the TeX assets to app storage on first use. Tiny, small, and balanced use compact ICU data; full also includes ICU encoding tables and line-break data.

Choose one variant. `full` includes every example-backed feature, not all of TeX Live. Excluding a feature omits files used only by its examples; shared dependencies remain. Add your own documents to the packing corpus before relying on additional packages.
